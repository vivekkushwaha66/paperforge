# Financial Statements Decomposition

Evaluating the three primary financial statements with automated variance extraction.

## Three-Statement Modeling

```text
Income Statement  ─── Net Income ───►  Cash Flow Statement
       │                                       │
  Depreciation                            Capex & NWC
       │                                       │
       ▼                                       ▼
Balance Sheet ◄── Retained Earnings & Cash Balance
```

### Key Accounting Metrics

* **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC):** $\text{NOPAT} / \text{Invested Capital}$
* **Free Cash Flow Conversion:** $\text{FCF} / \text{EBITDA} > 75\%$
* **Net Debt to EBITDA:** Target below $2.0\times$ for resilient balance sheet quality.
