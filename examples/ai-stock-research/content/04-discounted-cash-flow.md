# Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Modeling

The DCF model estimates the intrinsic value of a company based on projected future unlevered cash flows discounted to present value.

## Valuation Formula

$$\text{Enterprise Value} = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{\text{FCFF}_t}{(1 + \text{WACC})^t} + \frac{\text{Terminal Value}}{(1 + \text{WACC})^n}$$
