# Qualitative & Competitive Analysis

Qualitative equity research evaluates the durable competitive advantages (economic moats) of an enterprise.

## Porter's Five Forces Evaluation

| Competitive Force | Intensity | Key Drivers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Threat of New Entrants | Low | High switching costs, proprietary data flywheel |
| Bargaining Power of Buyers | Moderate | Enterprise contract multi-year lock-in |
| Bargaining Power of Suppliers | High | Specialized GPU / silicon supply constraints |
| Threat of Substitutes | Low | Deep mission-critical API integration |
| Industry Rivalry | High | Rapid architectural LLM evolution |
